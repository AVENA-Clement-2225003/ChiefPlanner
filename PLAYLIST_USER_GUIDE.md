# Playlist Feature - User Guide

## Quick Start

### Creating Your First Playlist

1. Click **"Playlists"** in the navigation menu
2. Enter a name in the "Nom de la playlist" field
3. Click **"Créer la playlist"**
4. Your playlist appears in the list below

### Adding Dishes to a Playlist

#### Method 1: During Dish Creation (Creators only)
1. Go to **"Plats"** page
2. Fill in the dish creation form
3. Check **"Ajouter à une playlist"**
4. Select your playlist from the dropdown
5. Click **"Ajouter le plat"**

#### Method 2: From All Dishes Page
1. Click **"Tous les plats"** in the navigation
2. Find the dish you want to add
3. Click **"Ajouter à une playlist"** button
4. Select your playlist from the modal
5. Click **"Ajouter"**

### Viewing a Playlist

1. Go to **"Playlists"** page
2. Click the **"Voir"** (eye icon) button next to any playlist
3. See all dishes in that playlist

### Managing Playlists

#### Rename a Playlist
1. Go to **"Playlists"** page
2. Click **"Modifier"** (pencil icon)
3. Enter new name
4. Click **"Sauvegarder"**

#### Delete a Playlist
1. Go to **"Playlists"** page
2. Click **"Supprimer"** (trash icon)
3. Confirm deletion
4. Note: This only deletes the playlist, not the dishes

### Removing Dishes from a Playlist

1. Open the playlist (click "Voir")
2. Click **"Retirer"** (trash icon) next to the dish
3. Confirm removal
4. Dish is removed from playlist but still exists in database

## Tips & Tricks

### Tip 1: Use Descriptive Names
Choose clear, descriptive names for your playlists to easily find them later.

### Tip 2: One Dish, Multiple Playlists
A dish can be in multiple playlists. For example, "Salade César" could be in both "Déjeuner" and "Repas rapides".

### Tip 3: Browse Before Creating
Check the "Tous les plats" page to see all available dishes before creating new ones.

### Tip 4: Regular Cleanup
Periodically review your playlists and remove dishes you no longer want.

### Tip 5: Plan Your Week
Create a "Cette semaine" playlist to plan your weekly meals.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I share playlists with other users?
A: Not in the current version. Each user has their own private playlists.

### Q: What happens to my playlists if I delete a dish?
A: The dish is automatically removed from all playlists when deleted.

### Q: Is there a limit to how many playlists I can create?
A: No limit! Create as many as you need.

### Q: Can I rename a playlist after creating it?
A: Yes! Click the "Modifier" button on the Playlists page.

### Q: What happens if I delete a playlist?
A: Only the playlist is deleted. All dishes remain in the database and in other playlists.

### Q: Can I add the same dish to multiple playlists?
A: Yes! A dish can be in as many playlists as you want.

### Q: How do I see all dishes in the database?
A: Click "Tous les plats" in the navigation menu.

## Troubleshooting

### Problem: Can't see the Playlists menu
**Solution:** Make sure you're logged in. The menu only appears for authenticated users.

### Problem: Playlist dropdown is empty when creating a dish
**Solution:** Create at least one playlist first from the Playlists page.

### Problem: Can't add a dish to a playlist
**Solution:** 
- Verify you own the playlist
- Check if the dish is already in the playlist
- Refresh the page and try again

### Problem: Changes not appearing
**Solution:** 
- Refresh the page (F5)
- Clear browser cache
- Check for error messages
